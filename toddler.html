Toddler

18 Months to 2.5 Years
1 Staff : 5 Children

Our industrious toddlers enjoy a fun and supportive environment that encourages exploration with a hands-on approach. Our educators celebrate toddlers growing sense of curiosity, independence, and expression. Our educators listen, observe and interact with each toddler to provide learning activities that will extend their learning. Our toddler program provides children opportunities for safe, supervised play, while encouraging their exploration and desire to test the boundaries of their abilities. Educators offer children a variety of creative, sensory, language, music and motor experiences. As social skills develop, educators support toddlers with their emerging capacity for interactive and cooperative play in a warm, positive environments.

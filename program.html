Our Programmes

  • To

    Toddler

    Our industrious toddlers enjoy a fun and supportive environment that encourages exploration with a hands-on approach. Our educators celebrate toddlers growing sense of curiosity, independence, and expression. Our educators listen, observe and interact with each toddler to provide learning activities that will extend their learning.

  • Pr

    Preschool

    Children are competent, capable, curious, and rich in potential. Our educators design environments, both inside and outside, that ignite curiosity, inquiry and investigation. They provide a wide variety of interesting objects and open-ended materials for children to explore.

  • Ki

    Kindergarten

    Our kindergarten program reflects the belief that four to six years old are capable and adept learners, full of potential, and equipped to take ownership of their learning. Our educators approach children as unique learners who live and learn within their families and communities.

  • SC

    Summer Camp

    Junior Hub is pleased to offer a summer camp program for the children aged 5 to 6, July 4th to September 2nd, 2022. Children will enjoy the numerous fun and educational indoor and outdoor games and activities that are great for summer. They will also explore the world of STEAM with hands-on Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Maths powered activities with our expert educators.

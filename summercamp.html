Summer Camp

5 - 6 years old
July 4 - September 2

Junior Hub is pleased to offer a summer camp program for the children aged 5 to 6, July 4th to September 2nd, 2022.

Children will enjoy the numerous fun and educational indoor and outdoor games and activities that are great for summer. They will also explore the world of STEAM with hands-on Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Maths powered activities with our expert educators.

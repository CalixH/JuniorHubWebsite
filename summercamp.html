Summer Camp

4 - 6 years old
July - September

Junior Hub is pleased to offer a summer camp program for the children aged 4 to 6, July to September. Register Here!

Children will enjoy numerous fun and educational indoor and outdoor games and activities that are great for summer. They will also explore the world of STEAM with hands-on Science, Technology, Engineering, Art and Maths powered activities with our expert educators.

Summer Camp Registration and Fees:

Item Price
Weekly 9:00am - 5:00pm $322
Extension Care 8:00am - 6:00pm +$40
Meal Plan Option (hot lunch and 2 snacks) +$30

Important Dates:

Registration is required for a minimum of 2 weeks. Registration date: February 1.
Camp will be closed on August 1st for the civic holiday (no charge on this day).
